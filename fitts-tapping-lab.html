Fitts Tapping Lab

Tap targets of different distance and width, then see how movement time follows the index of difficulty.

MT = a + b · log₂(D/W + 1)

Set up the session

Centre to centre. Comma separated.

Bar width or circle diameter.

Order

What happens
  1. Click the highlighted target to start a condition. That first click is not scored.
  2. Alternate between targets as fast as you can while staying accurate. Misses are counted but the trial still advances.
  3. Every condition is a D × W pair. Pairs that do not fit your window are dropped.
  4. Results give a regression of movement time on difficulty and effective throughput in bits/s.

Press Esc to abort a run. Use the same device and window size for every participant you compare.
