How it works
01

Find the closest target

Compare the pointer’s distance to each target boundary, not just its center.

02

Resize the activation area

The circle grows until it can contain the target — or until it would touch the second-closest target.

03

Morph when space is tight

If a circle cannot fully surround the target safely, a local envelope reinforces which target is captured.

Based on the original technique by Tovi Grossman and Ravin Balakrishnan, “The Bubble Cursor: Enhancing Target Acquisition by Dynamic Resizing of the Cursor’s Activation Area,” CHI 2005. This is an independent teaching demo, not the original software.
