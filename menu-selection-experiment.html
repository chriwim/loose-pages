Menu Selection Experiment

Find a target word in a menu hierarchy as quickly and accurately as you can. The same 64 words are arranged as deep menus with few choices, shallow menus with many choices, or one single screen.

How this version works

  • Task. A target word stays on screen. Click the menu item you think leads to it. In hierarchies, each click opens the next menu until the word itself appears.
  • Timing. The clock starts when the first menu appears and stops at your final click. Reading the target happens before you press Show menu.
  • Errors. A wrong choice ends the trial and counts as an error. Mean times use correct trials only.
  • Differences from the original. Every participant sees all structures (the original compared groups), responses are mouse or touch, and the 64 words are an original word set, not Miller's (1981) materials. RAN64 uses one random arrangement per session.
