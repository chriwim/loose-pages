Metric definitions
Gross WPM = completed word characters plus spaces ÷ 5 ÷ active minutes.
Net WPM = (completed characters − error keypresses) ÷ 5 ÷ active minutes, floored at zero.
Error rate = error keypresses ÷ letter/space keypresses × 100. Backspaces are tracked separately.
