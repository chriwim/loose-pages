Meteor Text Entry

Each meteor carries an English word. Type the word to destroy it. When too many meteors reach the ground the session ends. Entry speed and error rates are logged for every keystroke.

How these numbers are calculated

WPM counts (T − 1) characters per second × 60 ÷ 5, where T is the length of everything entered, spaces between words included. The clock runs from the first keystroke to the last word entered. Adjusted WPM is WPM × (1 − uncorrected error rate).

Keystrokes follow the Soukoreff and MacKenzie scheme: C correct characters, INF incorrect characters left in a submitted entry, IF incorrect characters that were typed and then deleted, F corrections (Backspace presses). Total error rate = (INF + IF) ÷ (C + INF + IF); corrected = IF ÷ (C + INF + IF); uncorrected = INF ÷ (C + INF + IF). KSPC = (C + INF + IF + F) ÷ (C + INF).

Letters typed toward a meteor that then crashed, with no other word matching, are cleared automatically and left out of these rates. They are listed as abandoned keystrokes in the export.
